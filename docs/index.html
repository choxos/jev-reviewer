    How it works

    Jev, TypeSafe's System One model, never writes an answer. It reads each file a few pages at a time and points at the lines that answer your question; the app copies those lines word for word, with their file and page.

    Every question takes two passes. First each stretch of every file is screened for all questions at once. Then each promising line is checked on its own, so a question comes back Reported, Unclear or Not found, and an answer spread over several table rows keeps all of them.

    Projects hold studies, and studies hold files and answers. All of it stays in this browser, on this device: nothing is uploaded or kept on a server. Only the text of the files and your questions go to TypeSafe, through this site's relay, which pays with a shared key up to a daily limit. In Chrome, speech recognition sends your voice to Google. This site counts visits with Google Analytics, which sees neither your files nor your questions nor the names of your projects and studies.

    You stay the reviewer. Every quote has three small buttons: the green tick checks it as the question's answer (it turns green, its words fill your answer below, and the other quotes fold away until you ask for them), the pencil opens its words in an editor for your answer (Done or Escape closes it, Cancel puts back what was there), and the squares copy it with its place. Your answer shows under the quotes, with its own pencil. The quotes stay word for word; your answer is what you edit. The Check button under a question checks it as it stands, for example when the files do not report it. The extraction table shows every study against every question, with what is reported, unclear, missing or checked, counts the PRISMA flow once studies are excluded (Exclude, under the file tabs, asks for a reason), exports one row per study or one row per quote, and compares your answers with a second reviewer's copy.

    Enter asks. Find looks up exact words in the files, at once and without Jev. Say a question, or “next” and “previous” to move between quotes.

    Keys, when no field is being typed in: j and k move between answers, c ticks or unticks the one in view, e edits its answer, n goes to the next answer not yet checked, and / goes to the question box. Once every answer is checked, the next study to check is one press away.

    Projects

    A project holds its studies; a study, its files and answers. They are kept in this browser, on this device, and never uploaded. A questions file loaded in a project runs on any of its studies, and the project's export has every study's answers in one sheet.
